How it works. 1) Pick Product photos or Banner photos. 2) Click Choose photo on any item, or drag a photo onto it. The photo is cropped to the right size automatically and shown straight away. 3) Click Download all as ZIP, unzip it into your website folder (it keeps the images/products and images/groups folders, so it replaces the old files), then re-upload the site. To change an image later, open this page again and replace just that item. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; all work happens in your browser.
